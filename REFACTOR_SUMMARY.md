# Structural consolidation

The final public project is Market Volatility Forecasting & Risk Control. A single canonical chain replaces public exploratory branches: adjusted daily OHLC, GK session plus aligned gap risk, monthly expanding HAR/GARCH/Rolling controls, direct five-day FHS, and a five-session Full VOL plus short-duration cash proxy application. The original 50% Core is secondary only.

Canonical FHS and scalar account functions were extracted from the final tail and Decision Layer v3 implementations. Import adapters now take explicit mainline inputs; they do not read retired research/artifact directories. The fixed models, lags, horizon, selection rules, normalization, cost and cash equations were not optimized or reselected. Marginal forecasts, FHS forecasts, target paths, account metrics and inference were compared with immutable final references before removal.

The earlier daily 15% application and experimental ES/probability/IEF allocation engines are retired; the selected application is the already completed Decision Layer Full VOL account with its original training-only normalization. There is no new strategy or backtest definition. The original weak cross-regime promotion result is preserved.

Twenty-three research decisions, including negative, weak and NOT RUN gates, are recorded privately. Only a short public history table remains. Canonical price inputs, forecasts, final HS/FHS and selected account references are protected under data/local; redundant original copies and old experiment outputs are removed after dependency checks. Source-level and file-level cleanup manifests precede deletion. External laboratories and the existing shadow remain outside the deletion scope.

README and REPORT are generated from canonical outputs. A fresh-output reproduction, mainline tests and public/import/link/fingerprint audits determine readiness; see VALIDATION.md and CLEANUP_REPORT.md for final measured status. No commit, remote publication, order or deployment is part of this consolidation.
