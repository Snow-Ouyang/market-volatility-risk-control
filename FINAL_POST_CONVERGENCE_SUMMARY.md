# Final post-convergence summary

**CANONICAL_MAINLINE = 5D_FULL_VOL. All historical evidence remains EXPOSED_HISTORY.**

Post-convergence extensions produced useful forecasting and diversification evidence, but none provided sufficiently stable portfolio-level increment to justify changing the canonical 5D architecture.

## What was learned

| Extension | Useful finding | Mainline decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gold / Equity–Gold | Gold risk is predictable; fixed strategic diversification had historical economic value | Separate research domain. Dynamic allocation and fixed-budget Gold/cash scaling did not establish robust incremental value |
| Defensive parking | Gold had positive average opportunity returns in actual de-risking windows | Gold remained a risky asset; IEF/TLT did not reliably beat cash. Keep the frozen 3M cash sleeve |
| Full VOL bands | Some fixed bands reduced trade counts | No broad stable region across notional turnover, risk overshoot and recent performance |
| 21D forecasting | Monthly risk is forecastable; simple HAR(1,5,22) improves on Rolling22 | Retain the forecasting finding; longer-lag, constrained and selector variants did not earn promotion |
| Adaptive 21D | Updating the forecast of remaining risk within a fixed interval helped | Reforecasting had value; path-surprise triggers and frequent parameter refits did not establish an independent decision rule |
| 5D–21D portfolio | Slower decisions can reduce turnover | Nearly collinear forecasts did not create independent fast/slow states or a robust matched-exposure portfolio increment |

## Why 5D remains the final choice

The retained pipeline connects daily adjusted OHLC, regular-session GK and overnight gap risk to aligned future-five-day holding risk, HAR(1,5,22), FHS and Full VOL with residual short-duration cash carry. Monthly expanding parameter refits, next-open execution, five-session rebalancing, the original cost/cash rules, no shorting and no leverage are unchanged.

SPY remains primary and QQQ a historical transfer check. Full VOL improves full-history risk control beyond a matched-average-exposure static account, but its return-timing contribution is weak or negative and the advantage does not hold in every regime. Positive forecast-loss point estimates do not override the frozen WEAK joint HAR-over-GARCH verdict. This remains reproducible historical risk research, not deployment approval.

Keeping 5D is an evidence-based model-selection decision. It does not imply that Gold diversification or longer-horizon forecasting failed, nor that their retained summaries are sufficient to reconstruct their deleted research accounts. A future separate project would need a newly authorized design and data audit.
